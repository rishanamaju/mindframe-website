OUR WHY ✿

Big change begins
with understanding.

MindFrame is a youth-led initiative dedicated to spreading awareness about mental health and brain health while supporting the fight against brain tumors.

We believe learning, sharing resources, and showing up for one another can help make these conversations feel more approachable. Through education, community activities, and fundraising, we’re working toward a future where brain health gets the attention and care it deserves.
